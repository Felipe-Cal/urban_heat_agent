# Gaia Heat Sync (2026 Prototype)

> **Role**: City Resilience Cockpit & Planetary Intelligence Proxy  
> **Stack**: Python, Streamlit, PyDeck, Mapbox (Simulated)  
> **Status**: Prototype / Agentic Spine Mockup

## 🌍 The Vision: Technical Evolution to 2040

This prototype represents the **"First Domino"** in a multi-stage roadmap towards a self-governing planetary intelligence.

### Stage 1: Local Urban Heat Agent (2026 - Current)
- **Scope**: Single Bio-Region (Los Angeles - Watts/Pacoima).
- **Function**: Aggregates thermal satellite data, IoT sensor readings, and biological asset (tree) health.
- **Agency**: "Human-in-the-loop." The agent identifies anomalies (Data Deserts, Heat Islands) and proposes interventions for the City Resilience Officer to approve.
- **Trust**: Verifiable Data Provenance (VDP) simulation for sensor data.

### Stage 2: Regional Orchestration (2032)
- **Scope**: Watershed & Energy-Grid scale.
- **Function**: Autonomous coordination between water districts, energy utilities, and urban forestry.
- **Agency**: "Semi-Autonomous." The system executes pre-approved micro-transactions (e.g., purchasing water rights for stressed canopy zones).

### Stage 3: Gaia Self-Sovereign AI (2040)
- **Scope**: Planetary.
- **Function**: A decentralized, hyper-reasoning entity managing the biosphere's homeostasis.
- **Agency**: "Sovereign." The AI holds legal personhood, owns funds, and contracts humans/robots for ecological maintenance.

## 🛠 Features of this Prototype

1.  **Bio-Minimalist UI**: A "Liquid Glass" aesthetic designed for high-trust institutional use.
2.  **The Biosphere (Map)**:
    *   **Thermal Inversion**: Heatmap based on Landsat simulation.
    *   **Nature ID**: Digital twins of individual trees with health status.
    *   **Sensor Grid**: IoT network density and gap analysis.
3.  **The Proxy (Sidebar)**:
    *   **Sense-Plan-Act Loop**: Live streaming log of the agent's reasoning process.
    *   **Scenarios**: "Deploy Nervous System", "Win-Win Intervention", "Verify Green Bond".
4.  **The Spine (Data Provenance)**:
    *   **Trust Badges**: Visual indicators of cryptographically signed data (VDP).
